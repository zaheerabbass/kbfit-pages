KB Fit

Resize an image to an exact file size in KB and exact pixel dimensions — for exam portals, visa sites, job applications and bank KYC forms.

What it does

  • Hits a stated size range in KB and exact pixel dimensions at the same time.
  • Works entirely on your device. Your images are never uploaded.
  • Crop and rotate before resizing — freehand, or locked to your photo’s own proportions.
  • Saves to your Downloads folder, with the option to save to your gallery or share directly.

Privacy

Privacy policy — the short version is that your images never leave your phone, and the app collects nothing about you. It is free and supported by ads, so Google collects the usual advertising data; the policy says exactly what and why.
